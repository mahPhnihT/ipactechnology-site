Our first product

We are in the process of building our product line. The first one is Tracue.

In development
Tracue

Tracue helps B2B companies reach their ideal customer profile (ICP). Define who you want to sell to, find the companies that fit, and know when they are ready to talk.

  • Filter companies by the criteria that define your ICP
  • Radar that surfaces accounts when a buying signal appears
  • Contacts unlocked only when there is a reason to reach out
  • Sources and timestamps kept for every data point
Visit tracue.com Request early access
Tracue Radar

Signal over noise

Built with AI

A data engine powered by Claude

Tracue uses large language models from Anthropic (Claude) to read and classify company websites, extract firmographics and public business contacts with their source, detect buying signals such as hiring, funding and product launches, and score how well each account fits your ICP. Our product is also developed with Claude Code.

  • ReadCompany websites, classified at scale
  • DetectBuying signals as they appear
  • ScoreICP fit for every account

How we build

01

Precision first

Fewer, better-fit accounts beat long lists. Our tools are built to narrow the field, not flood it.

02

Timing matters

The best moment to reach out is when a company shows intent. We build around signals, not guesswork.

03

Transparent data

Every data point should be traceable. We keep where it came from and when it was seen.
